SMART CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT PLATFORM

Do not double-click index.html. React/Vite websites must be opened through a
local web address rather than the file: protocol.

To open the presentation:

1. Double-click OPEN_PRESENTATION.cmd
2. Your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:4173/
3. Keep the small server window open while presenting.
4. Close that window when finished.

No backend, login, internet connection, or package installation is required
for the presentation page.
